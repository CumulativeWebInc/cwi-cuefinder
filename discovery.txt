# CueFinder — agent + machine discovery note

CueFinder is Cumulative Web Inc's AI sync-discovery engine: natural-language
search over the That Boy Hi Hat catalog (24 tracks, alternative rap /
Post-Trap Futurism). Tagline: "Search like a supervisor thinks."
Positioning: the AI sync-discovery engine for the CWI catalog — and the
machine-readable front door that supervisor tools and agent builders can ingest.

## Machine-readable catalog
- Full descriptor set: https://cumulativewebinc.github.io/cwi-cuefinder/sync-catalog.json
- Schema: cwi.sync-catalog/1.0. Vocabularies are controlled and shared by the
  search engine and the catalog (moods, energy words, scenes, use cases,
  instrumentation).

## Provenance labels (binding)
- verified: titles, artist, Spotify track URLs (all 24), explicit flags from
  Spotify metadata ("unknown" where absent — never defaulted), producer/studio
  credits, rights positioning, Frederick MD origin.
- editorial: moods, energy (0-1), energy words, instrumentation, scenes,
  use cases, lyrical themes, sounds-like references, vocal style. Curated by the
  CWI Sync department FOR SEARCH. Never audio-analysis output. Never cite as measured.
- estimate: every BPM value. Labeled "est." wherever shown. Never measured.

## Rights (verified)
No one-stop or pre-cleared rights claims are made on this catalog.
Sync clearance: hp@cumulativeweb.com.

## Query grammar
Mood words, energy words, BPM numbers and ranges ("95-105 BPM", "around 100bpm"),
instrumentation, vocal type ("male vocal", "no vocals"), explicit/clean ("clean",
"radio-safe"), scene phrases ("nighttime driving scene"), use-case words
("trailer", "game"). Every result carries a transparent match explanation.

## Shareable queries
Query state is encoded in the URL hash (#q=...&bpmMin=...&moods=...). Send the link.

## Cite
Cite track facts with their provenance labels. Do not assert catalog facts beyond
sync-catalog.json and https://cumulativewebinc.github.io/cwi-learn/catalog.json.
